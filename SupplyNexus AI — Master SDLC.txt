==================================================
PRODUCT CONTEXT
==================================================

SupplyNexus AI is intended to become a production-oriented, multi-tenant SaaS platform for Supply Chain Intelligence.

The long-term vision is to connect enterprise supply-chain systems and data such as:

- WMS
- ERP
- OMS
- TMS
- Inventory systems
- Warehouse systems
- Supplier systems
- Transportation systems
- SQL databases
- APIs
- CSV/Excel
- Documents
- S3/object storage

and provide secure AI-powered capabilities such as:

- Natural-language supply-chain querying
- Knowledge retrieval
- Data analysis
- Investigation
- Reasoning
- Recommendations

Examples:

“How many orders were returned?”
→ Data/SQL

“What is safety stock?”
→ RAG/Knowledge

“Why was SKU-10023 oversold?”
→ Agentic investigation

“Which warehouse has the highest backlog?”
→ Data analysis

==================================================
ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. Security first.
2. Multi-tenancy from the beginning.
3. PostgreSQL is the system of record.
4. Never give an LLM unrestricted database access.
5. Agents use governed tools.
6. MCP must provide governed AI access to capabilities.
7. Important AI operations must be observable and evaluatable.
8. Never allow cross-tenant data leakage.
9. Prefer simple architecture.
10. Do not over-engineer the MVP.
11. Major architectural decisions must be documented as ADRs.
12. AI must not replace deterministic software where deterministic logic is sufficient.
13. RAG, SQL/data access and agents are different capabilities and should not be treated as interchangeable.
